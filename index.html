Profile

From the bench to the public health laboratory

Ph.D. in Medical Biochemistry from Era’s Lucknow Medical College and Hospital, Era University (2026), with nine years across clinical biochemistry, molecular oncology research, teaching and the public health sector.

At the Centre for Health Research and Innovation (CHRI), a PATH affiliate in India, She worked as Public Health Coordinator to strengthen laboratory diagnostics and community based surveillance in collaboration with National Health Mission (NHM) and Directorate of Public Health, Odisha. Her research covers gene expression and gene polymorphism in prostate cancer and the metabolic setting around it.

She has taught MBBS, para-medical (BMLT, DMLT, BRIT, B.Pharm, D.Pharm, Nursing, Physiotherapy and Optometry) and M.Sc. Medical Biochemistry students, and knows laboratory establishment and accreditation in depth.
